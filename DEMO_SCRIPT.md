# AdLens PK — 3 Minute Demo Script

## The Setup (30 seconds)

Pakistani brand owners can spend Rs 50,000 per month on ads without knowing which competitors are active, which offers are saturated, or which creative patterns are working. AdLens PK turns that blind spend into a live intelligence feed: the current local snapshot contains 1,261 Pakistani ads across 12 normalized categories, led by Fashion with 382 ads (30.29%).

## The Demo (2 minutes)

**Step 1 — Open the app.** The Home dashboard loads immediately with 1,261 tracked ads, 525 page labels, and an 11.3% cash-on-delivery rate (142 ads). The judge sees Fashion as the largest category at 382 ads, then Electronics at 197; real advertisers include PhoneCase Pakistan with 44 captured ads, Bonanza.Satrangi with 20, and Zameen.com and Audionic - The Sound Master with 14 each. The hook chart makes the market signal clear: 1,085 ads (86.04%) use a Problem-Agitation hook, while 266 ads (21.09%) mention a price.

**Step 2 — Click Live Collection. Select Fashion. Click Start.** A browser opens to the public ad library, each scraped card receives a red glow as it is read, and the collection table progresses from Waiting to Running to Done while deduplicated Fashion ads are classified and saved. The live log shows the scraper moving through cards and the database counter tracks the newly saved records instead of treating repeated ads as new intelligence.

**Step 3 — Collection completes.** The Home dashboard refreshes with the newly saved data, the last-updated timestamp advances, and its category, COD, hook, advertiser, and collection totals update from the database. This makes each collection a measurable increment to the Pakistan-specific market snapshot rather than a static presentation.

**Step 4 — Navigate to Winning Formula. Select Fashion.** The screen is deliberately honest about the present snapshot: 0 Fashion ads qualify as 30+-day winners because the current `ad_records` dataset has not retained campaign-age history. Use the real leading signal as the next collection benchmark: among the 382 Fashion ads, 339 (88.74%) use Problem-Agitation hooks, only 6 (1.57%) offer COD, and 85 (22.25%) mention price. As repeated collections build durable age data, the same screen promotes the ads that survive 30+ days into a formula based on COD, CTA, price, hook, and copy length.

## The Close (30 seconds)

Every collection run expands the Pakistani dataset, and every relevance decision becomes feedback for a smarter classifier. AdLens PK gives Pakistani teams a growing, local competitor-intelligence advantage: see the live market, collect the evidence, and turn it into the next ad decision before competitors do.
